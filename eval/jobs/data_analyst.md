# Data Analyst

Coho Retail is looking for a Data Analyst to help merchandising and operations teams make decisions with data. New graduates are welcome to apply.

## Responsibilities
- Answer business questions with SQL and Python
- Build and maintain dashboards for store operations
- Present findings to non-technical stakeholders

## Required
- SQL
- Python or R for data analysis
- Experience building dashboards in Tableau, Looker or Power BI

## Preferred
- Statistics or A/B testing experience
- Advanced Excel
- dbt or other data modeling experience

Hybrid role, three days a week in our Atlanta office.
