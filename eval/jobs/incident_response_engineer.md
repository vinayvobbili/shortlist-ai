# Incident Response Engineer

Northwind Security Operations is looking for an Incident Response Engineer to lead investigations and improve how we detect and respond to threats.

## Responsibilities
- Lead end-to-end response to security incidents, from triage to postmortem
- Build and tune detections in our SIEM
- Automate response workflows
- Mentor analysts on investigation techniques

## Required qualifications
- 4+ years in security operations or incident response
- Experience leading incident investigations
- Hands-on experience with a SIEM such as Splunk or Microsoft Sentinel
- Scripting in Python for automation

## Preferred qualifications
- SOAR playbook development
- Malware analysis or reverse engineering (IDA Pro, Ghidra)
- Cloud security experience (AWS or Azure)
- GCIH, GCFA or OSCP certification

Must be a team player who thrives in a fast-paced environment. Benefits include health, dental, and a 401(k) match.
